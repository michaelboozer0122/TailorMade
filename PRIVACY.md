# TailorMade Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

TailorMade ("the app") helps you create versions of your resume tailored to specific jobs. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## What we process

- **Your resume.** This is the PDF you upload and the information in it, such as name, contact details, work history and education.
- **Job information.** This is the job link, job description or job title you enter.

## How it's used

- Your resume and the job information are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to our server. The server sends them to our AI provider, Anthropic, to read your resume and produce the tailored resume and cover letter.
- If you give a job link, our server downloads that public web page to read the job description.
- We use this data **only** to provide the app's features. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

## Where it's stored

- Your parsed resume and your tailored versions are stored **only on your device**.
- Our server does not save your resume or results after a request is finished.
- Anthropic processes requests under its commercial terms and does not use API data to train its models. See https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

## Deleting your data

Open **My Resume → Delete all my data** in the app, or uninstall the app. This removes everything stored on your device. We keep no copy.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
